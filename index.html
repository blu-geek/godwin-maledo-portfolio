About

Godwin Maledo

Godwin Maledo has built his career on one idea: everyone deserves a real shot at financial security, not just the wealthy. As Executive Marketing Director at World Financial Group, he equips families and corporations across Canada and the United States with the tools to reach both short-term and long-term financial goals — combining rigorous product knowledge with an unshakeable commitment to integrity and professionalism.

His long-term goal is to build an institution known for one thing above all: giving people credible, honest knowledge that changes the trajectory of their lives. It's the same philosophy behind everything he's known for — giving people a reason to dream again, empowering them with real financial security, and proving that anybody can win.
